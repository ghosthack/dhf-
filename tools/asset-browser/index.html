Asset Browser

Open the game's assets folder (for example app/src/main/assets), or drag it onto this page.

Everything is decoded locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Images (I), animations (A) and data tables (T: items, skills, quests, tips…) are decoded. Scripts (S) and UI (U) show their text. Music (B) and sound effects (W) play. Other folders use formats that are not decoded yet and are shown as hex and text.
