Asset Browser

Open the game's assets folder (for example app/src/main/assets), or drag it onto this page.

Everything is decoded locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Images (I), animations (A), data tables (T: items, skills, quests, tips…), object definitions (O), maps (M), palettes (P) and fonts (F) are decoded. Scripts (S) show their dialogue and commands; UI (U) shows its text. Music (B) and sound effects (W) play. Other folders use formats that are not decoded yet and are shown as hex and text.
